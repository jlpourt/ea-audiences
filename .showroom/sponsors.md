## Per-brand detail

**1. American Express (FINANCIAL).** On 2026-02-10 Amex announced a multiyear NBA/WNBA renewal that adds NBA Take-Two Media and lists "Reimagined NBA 2K in-game perks and experiences beginning with 2K27." On the official 2K27 Seasons page (Season 2), "AMERICAN EXPRESS 2XREP, NOVEMBER 7–8, Location: The City — Wear Amex apparel to earn 2XREP…". The archived 2K26 Seasons page (snapshot 2026-02-11) shows "AMERICAN EXPRESS 2XREP Jan 24, Location: Event Center (Gen 9) — Wear AMEX apparel…". Fan account 2KDispatch (2026-10-14, MEDIUM) reports the "American Express NBA Tip-Off takes over The Theater" from Oct 16, with exclusive Amex apparel for the first 200,000 players who play 3 Theater games.

**2. Gatorade (BEVERAGE).** The official 2K26 City Courtside Report says "2K26's City features an open-air plaza, with the Gatorade Training Facility, Event Center, Arena…". The official 2K27 edition contents include "25x 3 Types of Gatorade Boosts". Season event rewards include "Gatorade Boosts". The 2K27 Workout Warrior badge is unlocked by completing 12 Gatorade Training Facility workouts (RealSport101).

**3. CeraVe (LIFESTYLE / skincare).** Official 2K27 Seasons page: "THE CERAVE SPLASH ZONE, NOVEMBER 13–15, Location: Event Center… 3PT shots earn bonus points." Guide sites and a Steam forum report a CeraVe MyCAREER endorsement challenge. Business of Fashion (2026-09-04) and 2K's Ronnie Singh (LinkedIn) say "Coach, Bape, Hellstar, Starter and Cerave are all in The City this year." NBA.com (2025-10-06): CeraVe is the NBA's official skincare partner.

**4. Coach (APPAREL / luxury).** Archived official 2K26 Seasons pages show "COACH 2XREP Jan 17 — Wear Coach apparel and earn 2XREP…" and "COACH 2XREP Mar 14". 2K27: WWD's brand list, BoF and Ronnie Singh all confirm Coach in The City.

**5. adidas (FOOTWEAR).** Official 2K27 Seasons page: "ADIDAS 2XREP, OCTOBER 23–24 — Wear Adidas apparel…". Archived 2K26 page (2025-12-01): "Adidas 2XREP Dec 19, Event Center". 2K26 patch 1.010 fixed the Adidas 2XREP pop-up shops. WWD lists signatures from Anthony Edwards, James Harden, Damian Lillard, Derrick Rose (2K27 Ultra Edition cover athlete), Trae Young, T-Mac and Gilbert Arenas, plus Rivalry, Superstar and Top Ten.

**6. Nike (FOOTWEAR).** WWD (2026-07-22): Caitlin Clark is on the NBA 2K27 Deluxe cover wearing an unreleased Nike Caitlin 1 in "Yellow Zest". WWD (2026-09-05) says Nike has the most models, including every LeBron, Kobe, Kyrie, Ja, PG, Booker, Giannis, Sabrina and A'ja model, many KDs, the first in-game Caitlin 1, and retros (Air Foamposite One/Pro, Air Force 1, Air Max 1/90, Air More Uptempo, Dunk High).

**7. Kia (AUTO).** Archived official 2K26 Seasons pages show "KIA CHALLENGE Feb 20–22, Location: Event Center (Gen 9) — Compete in three different challenges: Trampoline Ball, Beat the Pros…" and "KIA CHALLENGE Apr 10–12". A 2K25 Kia Challenge ran in Jan 2025 (HoopsGamers).

**8. Converse (FOOTWEAR).** SBJ (2025-09-12) quotes NBA 2K GM Zak Armitage on working with Converse to show SGA's unreleased shoe on the 2K26 cover. WWD (2K27) lists the Shai 001, Chuck Taylor All Star low/high, Fastbreak, Pro Leather and Weapon.

**9. NVIDIA (TECH).** 2K newsroom launch release (2026-09-04): "NBA 2K27 supports NVIDIA DLSS 5… Developed in close collaboration between NVIDIA and Visual Concepts."

**10. State Farm (FINANCIAL).** Multiple 2025 YouTube guides cover the 2K26 "State Farm Endorsement" quest. Jake from State Farm's official TikTok (2025-09-05, "Returning to NBA 2K26") and X (2026-02-26, "Let me take my business back to NBA 2k26 @nba2k") promoted it. A fan site lists State Farm in 2K27 (unconfirmed).

**11. Reebok (FOOTWEAR).** Bleacher Report (2025-07-09) quotes Angel Reese: "Being on the cover of NBA 2K26 and debuting my first-ever signature shoe with Reebok on that cover, the Angel Reese 1…". WWD (2K27) lists the Angel Reese 1, Iverson and Shaq classics, Blast and Pump.

**12–15. Jordan Brand / Puma / New Balance / Under Armour (FOOTWEAR).** All from WWD's 2K27 sneaker breakdown (2026-09-05). 2K27 has 17 athletic footwear brands, the same as 2K26. Others listed: Anta, Li-Ning, Peak, Rigorer, Qiaodan, 361, Skechers, 741.

**16. BAPE (LIFESTYLE).** Listed by WWD (2K27 apparel slate) and named by BoF and Ronnie Singh.

**Streetwear slate (2K27, WWD; "subject to change with new limited-edition drops"):** 47 Brand, Anti Social Social Club, Bape, Birth Of a Royal Child, BucketSquad, Chalk Line, Coach, Culture Kings, DannijoPro, Denim Tears, Diet Starts Monday, Ed Hardy, Essentials, Fried Rice, gvgallery, Hell Star, HMDD, Homage, iSlide, Jon Stan, Joshua Samuels, LaFrance, Lost Shadows, Nyrva, OVO, Paper Planes, Playa Society, Pro Standard, Revenge, Round 21, Sinclair, Slam, Star Chain, Starter, The Edge of Earth, Trapstar, True Religion, Vale Forever, Vandy The Pink, Visionary Club, Washed Dads, Wild Collective.

**2K26 new brands (SBJ, 2025-09-12):** Culture Kings, Denim Tears, Diet Starts Monday, ESSENTIALS, Fried Rice, Gvgallery, Hidden NY, HMDD, Kids of Immigrants, KidSuper, Lost Shadows, Playa Society, Rhone, Rivington, Star Chain, Trapstar, True Religion, WNTD (Bluboy). Billionaire Boys Club returned after missing 2K25.

**Non-NBA:** Minute Maid × WWE (Marketing Dive, 2025-04-09): "in-game integrations in WWE 2K25". PGA TOUR 2K25 (SBJ, 2025-02-07): Sun Day Red and Lululemon added to a 28-brand apparel/equipment roster.
